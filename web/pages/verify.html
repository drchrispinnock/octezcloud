Verify attestation

Paste the attestation document and public key that your deployment outputs. Every check runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Not available yet

The verifier ships with milestone M2. It will check:

  1. The certificate chain or token signature against the cloud's root of trust (AWS Nitro root CA, Google Confidential Space JWKS, Microsoft Azure Attestation signing keys).
  2. That the measurement (PCR0, image digest or CCE policy hash) matches a published, signed release.
  3. That the public key is bound to the attestation.
  4. That debug mode is off.

A CLI with the same checks will be published for offline verification.
